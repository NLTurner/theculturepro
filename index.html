The question before the solution

Something isn’t working.

The hard part is not always knowing that there is a problem. It is knowing what is actually producing it.

Organizations often respond to symptoms with more training, new initiatives, restructuring, process changes, or another strategy. Sometimes those are the right answers. Sometimes they are expensive responses to the wrong problem.

•
Our strategy makes sense. Execution doesn't.
•
We keep addressing the issue. It keeps coming back.
•
The organization has changed, but the way we operate hasn't.
•
We know something isn't working. We're not sure why.

Your issue may not fit neatly into any of these examples. That's the point. We start with what your organization is experiencing, not with a predetermined solution.

Inside-Out Transformation Framework™

Results have a history.

What an organization produces is shaped by more than its strategy or stated intentions. The Culture Pro® looks across the layers that influence how work is actually understood, carried out, reinforced, and measured.

01

Mindset

Beliefs, assumptions, interpretations.

02

Behavior

Choices, habits, interactions.

03

Systems

Processes, structures, incentives.

04

Strategy

Direction, priorities, decisions.

05

Results

What the organization actually produces.

Lasting results are created from the inside out.

The framework helps organizations look beyond the result itself and trace it back through the conditions that are producing it. A performance issue may appear in the outcome, while its source lies in the assumptions guiding decisions, the behaviors those assumptions create, the systems reinforcing those behaviors, or the way strategy is translated into action. Examining the layers together makes it possible to identify where change is actually needed rather than repeatedly trying to correct the result at the end of the chain.

Nicole L. Turner is an organizational effectiveness strategist, executive advisor, speaker, author, and business professor known for seeing across the parts of an organization that are too often examined separately.

Nicole brings a multidisciplinary perspective shaped by experience across strategy, operations, organizational effectiveness, leadership, change, technology, business, and entrepreneurship. Her perspective is also sought beyond traditional client engagements. Nicole advises and mentors entrepreneurs, evaluates emerging ventures and established businesses, and contributes to university entrepreneurship and innovation ecosystems.

That way of thinking led her to create the Inside-Out Transformation Framework™, which examines how mindset, behavior, systems, and strategy combine to shape results.
