The question before the solution

Something isn’t working.

Sometimes the problem is clear. What’s causing it isn’t.

Organizations come to us for many reasons. These are some of the situations that can signal a deeper organizational issue.

A major initiative or investment isn’t producing the expected result.
Growth has exposed problems the organization wasn’t built to handle.
The strategy makes sense. Execution isn’t following.
The organization has changed, but the way work gets done hasn’t.
The same issue keeps returning despite repeated attempts to fix it.
Performance should be better, but leadership hasn’t determined what’s creating the gap.

That’s why we diagnose before we prescribe.

How we think

We don’t prescribe before we diagnose.

Organizations operate as systems. What appears to be a leadership issue may be rooted in structure. What looks like an execution problem may begin with unclear strategy. A performance issue may involve processes, capabilities, technology, decision-making, or several conditions working together.

The Inside-Out Transformation Framework™ provides a disciplined way to look beyond symptoms and understand what is creating the result.
