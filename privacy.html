Privacy Notice

Effective September 13, 2026

The Culture Pro® respects your privacy. This notice explains the information that may be collected when you use this website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Those providers may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser or device information, request data, and security-related information, as part of operating, protecting, and delivering the website.

Sharing of information

The Culture Pro® does not sell personal information. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, or to meet legal and operational requirements.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit information through the website. If you have previously submitted an inquiry and would like to ask about the information associated with it, you may contact The Culture Pro® through the Start a Conversation page.

Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated as the website, services, or technology providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the current version.

Contact
